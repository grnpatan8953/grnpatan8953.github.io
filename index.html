Retail floor work, made practical

Clear boards.
Confident floor walks.

AUREM is a Windows app for supermarket teams to check products and prices, manage floor walks, prepare promotional boards, and create print-ready PDFs.

Core workflows are designed to work locally on your device.

One workspace for the shop floor

AUREM brings routine retail preparation together, from importing store reports to reviewing boards before printing.

01 / CHECK

CORE & NON-CORE

Use store reports to scan products, review stock and selling prices, and organize floor-walk findings.

02 / PREPARE

Boards & audits

Build offer boards, round boards and custom layouts, and keep product, rack and category details organized.

03 / PRINT

Review & export

Preview board layouts and save PDFs for manual review and printing at the intended physical size.

Work stays in your hands.

Operational records are stored locally by the app. Optional AI tools and phone-to-PC transfers use network connections only when those features are used.
