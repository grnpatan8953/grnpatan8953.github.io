Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy describes the current AUREM Windows app behavior: Google identity sign-in, locally stored operational records, optional AI services, and optional phone-to-PC transfers.

1. Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, AUREM requests the OpenID Connect scopes openid, email and profile. Google processes the sign-in and returns identity information such as your email address, name and profile image. AUREM's local backend exchanges the authorization response with Google and may receive access and refresh tokens as part of that flow.

The app stores the sign-in session on the Windows device under the current Windows user's local application data. The session contents, including any refresh token received, are protected with Windows user-scoped DPAPI encryption. The browser interface receives sign-in status and profile display fields; it does not receive the stored refresh token. Signing out removes AUREM's local session file. It does not itself revoke AUREM's permission in your Google Account; you can manage that permission through Google Account settings.

Google Sign-In identifies an account. It does not by itself grant access to paid or billable AI services.

2. Information stored on your device

AUREM stores operational app data in browser IndexedDB on the device, including imported or entered product information, floor-walk selections, saved boards, audit details and print-related records, depending on the features you use. AUREM's local backend also keeps app configuration and the encrypted sign-in session in the current Windows user's local application data.

Signing out of Google does not erase operational records. Those records remain on the device until removed through available app controls or the relevant browser/app storage is cleared. Local browser storage is separate from the DPAPI-protected sign-in session; this policy does not claim that all operational records are encrypted.

3. Optional AI features

Some creative, replica and matching features can send information to an AI service when you choose to use them. Depending on the feature, this can include the prompt or instructions you enter, selected product or catalog context, and reference images you upload or create. The current app routes AI requests through an operator-configured private gateway or locally available processing where supported. A configured gateway may pass request content to the AI provider it uses.

These requests are separate from Google Sign-In. Review the applicable AI service and provider terms before submitting business information or images. AI availability may require an authorized service and may incur usage charges. AUREM does not put private AI provider credentials in the browser interface.

4. Phone-to-PC transfers

If you pair a phone with a PC and send a finished floor walk, the selected floor-walk data is transmitted over the local network to the paired AUREM PC. The transfer is an optional workflow initiated by the user. Keep the pairing link or QR code private and use it only with a device you trust.

5. Network connections and the public website

Google Sign-In communicates with Google. Optional AI requests communicate with the configured AI service. A phone-to-PC transfer communicates with the paired PC. Core local workflows do not require a Google account.

These public information pages are static HTML and do not include AUREM forms, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or third-party page assets. If hosted on GitHub Pages or another hosting service, that host may process ordinary website request information under its own policies.

6. Retention and choices

  • Use AUREM's sign-out control to delete the local encrypted Google session.
  • Use the app's data controls, where available, or clear the app's local browser storage to remove locally stored operational records. Export or back up anything you need first.
  • Manage or revoke AUREM's Google permission in your Google Account settings.
  • Do not submit confidential or personal information to optional AI features unless you are authorized to do so.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when AUREM's behavior changes. The date above identifies the latest published revision. The applicable handling of an optional third-party service is also governed by that service's own terms and privacy information.
